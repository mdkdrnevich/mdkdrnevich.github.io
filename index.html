About

I've spent most of my life asking how things work, and then trying to teach computers to find the answers. This December I'm joining the Applied AI team at Everpure, where I'll work with companies to take AI from a promising idea to something that holds up in production.

Before that, I spent six years building machine learning tools for particle physics on CERN's ATLAS experiment while doing my Ph.D. in Physics at NYU with Kyle Cranmer, with a detour to Hamburg as a fellow at Helmholtz AI. Most recently, I built generative models for 3D electromagnetic simulation as a founding researcher at Root Access.
