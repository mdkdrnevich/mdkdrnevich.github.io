01Hello

I'm a Consulting Applied AI Engineer on Everpure's Applied AI team. I work with enterprise customers and partners to find high-value AI use cases, design end-to-end deployments, and build proof-of-value prototypes that connect technical performance to business outcomes.

Before this, I spent six years as a research scientist on the ATLAS Collaboration at CERN, where I built probabilistic machine learning methods that went into production simulation workflows. They cut storage by up to 95% and compute cost by up to 109×. Most recently I was an AI Research Fellow at Root Access, leading work on physics-informed generative models for 3D electromagnetic simulation.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from NYU, advised by Kyle Cranmer. I was also a visiting fellow at Helmholtz AI (DESY, Hamburg). Before that I studied physics and mathematics at Notre Dame.
